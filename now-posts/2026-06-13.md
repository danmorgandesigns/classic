# June 13, 2026

Whew, a lot has happened since April. I shipped my final update to my international visitor’s guide to Kansas City, named the Show Me KC Guide on the iOS App Store. It was a nice labor of love to my current “hometown” that I’m also glad is completed.

In April I pivoted HARD. I decided to pursue a crazy idea of opening a downtown pool hall in the city of my alma mater, Manhattan, KS, home to Kansas State University. It IS a crazy idea that now is a real project spending real money on the business plan, architect’s drawings, and early marketing plans. I’m relocating from Overland Park to Manhattan later this summer with plans to begin construction in January and opening in August 2027.
