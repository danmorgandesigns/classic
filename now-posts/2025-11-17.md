# November 17, 2025

Version 1.1 of the Arts and Rec Guide is up on the Apple App Store. Very pleased with the results. I recreated some of the features in a website so Android users would at least have some access to the guide. I'm pausing on futher development until spring when the gardens green up and water features are flowing again

My trail route and field mapping utility, Gretel is getting close to a feature-complete state. I'm waaayy past MVP at this point and probably in a feature-creep state, in reality. I'm still debating about adding photo capture/storage/sync/sharing support or not. 

I had quite lot of fun learning a little bit about 8-bit animation just to create a short explainer video. It really doesn't serve a practical purpose except spark a little joy and whimsy.
