# October 14, 2025

I submitted the the app Overland Parks Arts & Rec Guide to the App Store for final approval. I got it to the external Test Flight stage yesterday and pushed it all the way this afternoon.

I've already started on my second iOS app I'm calling "Gretel". It is primarily a utility for me to map trail routes and drop pins for points of interest (POIs) with meta data. It allows the user to export the routes as individual geoJSON files and the POIs as a single JSON file of objects. These files then go into the Arts & Rec Guide as mapped trails and points of interest.

Why? I hated the way I had to map the trails and record the GPS locations for the Arts & Rec app, so I decided to build a better tool.
