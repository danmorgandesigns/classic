Gretel: The Trail Route & POI Mapping App

Exporting trail route and points of interest out of commerical hiking apps is cumbersome and unintuitive. This app is focused on mapping a trail route and marking points of interest along the way. It allows the export of data in a variety of file formats such as geoJSON, Google Earth KML/KMZ, GPX Route, etc.

Special features include the ability to "dial in" the minimum spacing between points in your saved route geometry (Route Precision) and change the minimum movement before a new location update is delivered while tracking (Update Frequency).

Still in development.
