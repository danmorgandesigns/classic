# Is this Software Development's 'No Synths Used' Moment?

Years ago, I remember reading the liner notes in Boston's second album "Don't Look Back" (1978). (Remember liner notes? Remember records?)

Tom Schulz, the guitarist and driving force in Boston has his masters from MIT in mechanical engineering. In addition to being a rock star he's also an inventor and has multiple patents to his name. To me, reading his liner notes is always a good time.

Two lines stuck out to me from that album:
"No synthesizers used. No computers used"

I think we're at a "no synths used" moment in software development. The folks who are taking a very strident "No AI in MY software development!" stand will look quaint to the synth players happily coding along with their AI agent looking over their shoulders. Yes there will be the over-use of Autotune, meaning there'll be some really bad code and buggy software, but meanwhile the industry will race onward.

You may stomp your feet and say you like the warmth and tone of your vinyl software development, it will become an affectation instead of the norm.
