# Sunday Ritual
## November 9, 2025

There was a really good segment on Billy Bob Thornton today. He recounted a conversation he had early in his career with famed director Billy Wilder. Thornton quoted him saying:

"Write your own stories, create your own characters. Don’t stand in line with everyone else.”
