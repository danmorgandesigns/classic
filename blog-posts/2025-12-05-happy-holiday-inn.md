# Happy Holiday Inn
## December 5, 2025

This commercial art reproduction veers ever slightly to parody. The old Holiday Inn signs still fill me with a certain joy—an anticipation of travel and hotel pools as a child. I don't think I ever stayed at a classic Holiday Inn as a kid, but I have stayed in a lot of Holidomes and Expresses since then. They really should bring back the classic signs.
