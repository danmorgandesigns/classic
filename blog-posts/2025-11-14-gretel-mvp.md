# Website Version - Arts & Rec Guide
## November 14, 2025

I'm close to an MVP state for my trail and point of interest mapping tool, Gretel. It went from being a quick and dirty utility for me to map trails and drop pins to something commercial, targeted at people who create and maintain hiking trails and parks.
I got to thinking about video tutorials and while hiking, naturally, I had the idea of animating my mascot as an 8-bit sprite showing what the app is for.
