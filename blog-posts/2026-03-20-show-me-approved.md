# Show Me KC Guide Approved
## March 20, 2026

The Show Me KC Guide is available on the iOS App Store. It is a curated guide covering 16 itineraries, over 80 locations, and translated into seven languages—created for the international visitor coming to Kansas City for this summer's World Cup.
