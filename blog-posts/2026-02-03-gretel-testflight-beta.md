# Gretel TestFlight Beta
## February 3, 2026

Yesterday, I submitted my trail and point-of-interest mapping tool, Gretel, to the Apple App Store for external TestFlight approval. 

I recently added the ability to attach a photo to a trail route or POI upon creation or in edit mode. This will enhance the ability for a park or trail person to record and share hazards or invasive species with co-workers.

Hopefully the weather improves so I can more completely field-test the app and share the beta soon.
